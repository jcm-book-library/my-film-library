Film Vault · theme proposal

Twelve films, twelve months

Each theme borrows colour, type and shading from a film you picked. Nothing here copies a still, poster or logo: the look is built from colours, fonts and CSS texture only, and the films are made up. The app would open in the month's theme, with a switcher to flick between them and a plain Default.

    How to give feedback
    1. Scroll the cards (or jump with the row below). Each mock-up uses only that theme's colours and fonts.
    2. Tell me in chat what to change, in plain words: “Matrix: green too bright”, “Totoro: swap the red for yellow”, “Hateful Eight: no stripes”.
    3. Say if a month should swap with another.
    4. The small badges show text contrast against the panel colour. 4.5 or more reads comfortably; anything lower is flagged in red.
