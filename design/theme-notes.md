# Theme notes (inspiration only: no stills, posters or logos go in the app)

Decisions so far: app opens in THIS MONTH's film theme; Default (near-black/dark grey, light highlights, blue #8fb8f2, no colour drift) is the plain option;
month mapping to be agreed once all screenshots are in; theme edits editor in Settings (colours, fonts from a short Google Fonts list, roundness,
grain/glow, light/dark, reset, save as copy); edits saved per device for now (sheet sync later); reel icon + "Film Vault" text in the header.

## 1. Jurassic Park (3 images: gate/jeep/T. rex in rain, rotunda banner, black credits)
Colour (by eye): deep jungle greens (dark forest ~#1f3321, foliage mid ~#4d7a3a); torch/fire orange (~#e8532a); logo red-orange (~#d4372a) with
gold/yellow outline (~#f2c230); jeep yellow-green; khaki/rose shirt (~#c9695c); rainy night blue-grey (~#2b3a40, cold light, wet black asphalt);
rotunda warm amber/tan interior (~#c98a5a) with glowing sunset window; credits: pure black with burnt orange (~#f08a1c) heavy letters.
Type: logo = flared, chunky serif capitals in a red-to-orange gradient with a gold keyline (look-alike candidates: Alfa Slab One / Abril Fatface / Rye);
credits = heavy rounded-square sans, all caps, wide letter-spacing (look-alikes: Bungee / Titan One / Passion One).
Shading: dark vignette, rain-soaked contrast, soft VHS-like glow on credit text, warm firelight spill, banners with gold keylines.
Likely dark theme: black/dark-forest ground, orange accent, gold highlights, green secondary.

## 8. 12 Angry Men (2 images: black-and-white modern poster with a double-exposed title; 1957 yellow poster with a knife). Owner note: a throwback to old Hollywood in general.
Colour (by eye): the modern poster is monochrome: soft mid-grey studio gradient (~#8a8a8a lightening to ~#c8c8c8 at the top, darker ~#3a3a3a at the corners), crisp white shirts (#f4f4f2), near-black ties and type (~#111);

Likely a LIGHT theme (greys and white with black type, hard shadows) with a yellow-and-black "1957 poster" accent; possibly a dark noir variant (black, white, grey).
